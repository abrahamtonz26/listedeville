Tony Abraham

Technology entrepreneur. New Yorker. A passionate home cook who learned that the best way to understand a dish is to try to recreate it the next morning.

Honestly Speaking is written from a simple conviction: that the food itself — its taste, its texture, its quiet decisions — deserves a more careful kind of attention than it usually gets. The plate, not the room. The dish, not the hype.

Based in New York, with field notes from all around. listedeville / New York is the first published edition of the city guides — a running list of the places worth an evening, kept the way a New Yorker keeps one: revised, argued over, and never quite finished.

Notes & sources

How the list is made

Every place is cross-referenced against Yelp, Google, The New York Times, OpenTable, Resy, The Infatuation, Eater, Time Out and the Michelin Guide, then argued down to the best of the best — must-visit, unique, a rockstar in its own right. Each list runs to at least fifty places; restaurants and bars to well over a hundred. First compiled four years ago and rebuilt for Fall/Winter 2026.

Booking and the Amex Resy credit

Each place carries a booking platform — Resy, Tock, OpenTable, SevenRooms or Walk-in — as best known at time of publishing. Platforms change; the app is the source of truth.

Amex ✓ marks places where the American Express Resy dining credit should apply: the Platinum Card earns up to $100 back each quarter and the Gold Card up to $50 twice a year at U.S. Resy restaurants, paid with an enrolled card — no booking through Resy required. Since August 1, 2026, eligible venues carry a "Resy Credit eligible" badge in the Resy app, and Tock venues join the credit from September 15, 2026 as Tock folds into Resy. Check the badge before you commit a credit.

Occasions and James Beard

Occasion chips — Romantic, Date night, Casual, Group / lively, Brunch & daytime, Late night, Coffee & sweets — come from the companion Honestly Speaking — New York Guide by Occasion (Fall/Winter 2026), which sorts the guide's open venues by how you'd use them. James Beard marks are the 2026 nominations for New York: Corima, Meju, Shukette, Claud and Carnitas Ramírez for Best Chef; The Four Horsemen for Outstanding Restaurant; Lei for Best New Restaurant; Clemente Bar for Best New Bar; Ramen by Rā (Emerging Chef); Chambers (wine); Borgo and Whoopsie Daisy for beverage and cocktail service.

Verify before you go

Anything marked verify is a place whose current status couldn't be confirmed for this edition, or that has been reported closed, relocated or renamed. A quick check on Resy or Google before planning an evening around it is worth the thirty seconds.
